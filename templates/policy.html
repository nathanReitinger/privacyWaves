The short version: we need very little to run a shop, we ask before taking anything else, and everyone who receives your data is named below.

At a glance

  • Who we are. Panopti Ltd, 4 Pocklington Yard, London EC1A 4XY. Data protection officer: dpo@panopti.example.
  • What we always collect. Your session, your cart, and basic device and network data. Needed to serve the page and keep it secure.
  • What we only collect if you agree. Analytics, advertising, profiling, and sharing with partners. All off unless you switch them on.
  • Who receives it. Six organisations, named in section 4, with what each one gets and why.
  • How long. Six years for order records because tax law says so. Everything optional is 13 to 14 months.
  • Your rights. Section 7, and every one of them works from the Your rights tab without emailing anyone.

1. Who is responsible

Panopti Ltd is the controller for the processing described here. Our data protection officer can be reached at dpo@panopti.example or at the postal address above. You can contact the DPO about anything in this notice, and you do not have to go through us before complaining to a regulator.

3. Strictly necessary processing

Some processing happens whatever you choose, because the site cannot work without it: keeping your session, remembering your cart, balancing load, preventing fraud, and recording what you consented to so we can prove it. We keep this category as narrow as we can, and we do not use it to smuggle in analytics or advertising. If you reject everything else, this is all that runs.

4. Who receives your data

Article 13(1)(e) of the GDPR requires us to tell you the recipients, or the categories of recipients, of your personal data. The European Data Protection Board's transparency guidelines say the default should be actual names, and that a controller using categories instead must still say what type of organisation, in what industry and sector, and where. We have named them.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Two recipients are outside the EEA, and both are named above with the mechanism that covers them: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for NorthBeam Audiences, and standard contractual clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) plus a transfer impact assessment for Veridia Risk Signals. A copy of the clauses is available from the DPO on request.

6. Automated decision-making

If you consent to profiling, we order collections by predicted relevance and choose which advertisement to show. This has no legal or similarly significant effect on you, so Article 22(1) does not apply, but you can see the inputs and ask a person to review the outcome from the Your rights tab.
